Chronos

4,150 years, 157 lives, one story.

Every bar is a life, from Adam to the resurrection. See who lived at the same time, how the generations connect, and how the story's threads rise and fall across the centuries.

How to explore

  • Tap a bar to read about that person, with the scripture behind them and everyone alive alongside them.
  • Click or tap an empty part of the timeline to see who was alive that year. Drag across it with a mouse, or along the date strip with a finger, to move through time.
  • Search for a person, a year like 586 BC, or an era. ⌘K or Ctrl K jumps to search.
  • Pinch, ⌘-scroll or Ctrl-scroll to zoom, and press Whole story to see everything again. The arrow keys step through people or years, and Esc clears your selection.
  • Every selection has its own address, so you can share what you're looking at.

Timeline, Lineage and Narrative

Timeline shows lifespans and reigns. Lineage follows the generations, with the Messianic line through the center. Narrative's ribbons show editorial emphasis across the story, not measured quantities.

How the dates work

Dates before the monarchy follow James Ussher's Annals of the World (1650). Divided-kingdom dates follow Edwin R. Thiele's The Mysterious Numbers of the Hebrew Kings (1983). These are particular chronologies, not a scholarly consensus.

Prophets' lifespans and some genealogical dates are estimates. For some kings, the reign stands in for an unknown birth and death. The judges are shown one after another, though many likely overlapped.
